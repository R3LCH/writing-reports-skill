# Writing Reports

Use `skills/writing-reports-pandoc-gost/SKILL.md` for Russian GOST-style student, laboratory, and course reports and strict DOCX/PDF outputs.

## Core Rules

- Clarify target output and formatting authority before generating.
- Identify template roles, credentials, source data, and acceptance checks.
- Never invent credentials or source content.
- For paired GUAP DOCX/PDF, prefer a native editable Word master, a separately filled cover template and PDF exported from the finalized DOCX; read `skills/writing-reports-pandoc-gost/references/word-master.md`. Use the pinned LaTeX route when explicitly needed. Pandoc/Quarto are optional preparation tools.
- Preserve source/build inputs beside outputs and verify the final document.

## GOST Defaults

When no stricter rule is provided: A4; margins 30/15/20/20 mm (GOST 7.32–2017); Times New Roman 14 pt; 1.5 line spacing; first-line indent 1.25 cm; justified body text; black body text; title page counted without visible number; plain monospaced listings. Verify PDF and rendered DOCX independently.
